# Engineerign-Tranparent-Skill
Here’s a list of **transparent skills** (i.e., clearly observable, measurable, and demonstrable) that are valuable for a **software engineer** in development. These skills can typically be shown through projects, portfolios, codebases, contributions, or daily work habits:

---

### 🧠 **Core Programming Skills**

1. **Proficiency in Programming Languages**
   (e.g., JavaScript, Python, Java, C++, Go, Rust)

2. **Clean Code Practices**
   (Readable, maintainable, and well-structured code)

3. **Data Structures & Algorithms**
   (Using the right tools for performance and logic)

4. **Version Control Systems**
   (e.g., Git, GitHub/GitLab workflows)

---

### 🌐 **Web & App Development**

5. **Frontend Development**
   (HTML, CSS, JS, React, Vue, etc.)

6. **Backend Development**
   (Node.js, Express, Django, Laravel, Spring Boot, etc.)

7. **Database Design & Querying**
   (SQL – MySQL/PostgreSQL, NoSQL – MongoDB/Firebase)

8. **RESTful API & GraphQL Design**

9. **Authentication & Authorization**
   (JWT, OAuth2, session management)

---

### 🛠️ **Tools & DevOps**

10. **Testing & Debugging**
    (Unit, integration, end-to-end testing – e.g., Jest, Cypress)

11. **CI/CD Pipelines**
    (GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Jenkins, etc.)

12. **Docker & Containerization**

13. **Cloud Deployment**
    (AWS, GCP, Azure, Vercel, Netlify)

---

### 📐 **Software Architecture & Practices**

14. **System Design**
    (Scalability, fault tolerance, load balancing)

15. **Design Patterns**
    (Singleton, Factory, Observer, etc.)

16. **Agile & Scrum Practices**

17. **Documentation Writing**
    (API docs, README, architecture decisions)

---

### 📊 **Professional & Collaborative**

18. **Code Review Participation**

19. **Task Management Tools**
    (JIRA, Trello, Notion, Asana)

20. **Open Source Contributions**

21. **Pair Programming**

22. **Communication & Reporting**

---
